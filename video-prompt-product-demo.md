# CV Central — Product Demo Video Prompt

A general-purpose text-to-video prompt for generating a ~30-second product demo. Works with Sora, Runway, Veo, Kling, or similar tools — paste the whole block, or trim scene-by-scene if your tool generates in short clips.

---

## Master prompt

A sleek 30-second product demo video for "CV Central," an AI-powered CV builder website. Dark, modern tech aesthetic — near-black background (#0A0A14), deep violet (#5B2D8E) and electric lime-green (#AAFF00) as the only accent colors, clean sans-serif UI typography, soft ambient glow lighting, subtle floating particle/neural-network line accents in the background. Smooth, confident camera moves — slow push-ins and gentle parallax, no shaky cam. Screen-recording style close-ups of a laptop/phone screen showing a polished web app UI, intercut with clean minimal motion graphics. Pacing is brisk and modern, cut roughly every 2-3 seconds, in time with an upbeat, minimal electronic music track with a soft rising synth swell toward the end.

Scene 1 (0:00-0:04): Open on a dark screen. A cursor types a job title into a clean search-style input box. Bold white text fades in above it: "Still sending the same CV to every job?" Violet glow pulses softly behind the input box.

Scene 2 (0:04-0:09): Cut to a laptop screen showing a CV builder interface — a live radial progress ring in violet filling up from 0 to 78, with the number counting up in a bold monospace font. Small lime-green checkmarks tick off next to a list of "next steps" as the ring fills. Text overlay: "Score your CV like a recruiter would."

Scene 3 (0:09-0:15): The screen shows AI-generated bullet points appearing letter-by-letter (typewriter effect) on a CV preview, transforming a plain rough sentence into a polished, quantified achievement bullet with a subtle lime-green highlight sweeping across the new text. Text overlay: "AI rewrites your experience — instantly."

Scene 4 (0:15-0:21): Quick montage of 3-4 different CV template thumbnails sliding past in a horizontal grid, each with a distinct color accent (violet, teal, navy, orange), a violet border glow highlighting one as it gets selected with a satisfying snap animation. Text overlay: "Pick a template built for your industry."

Scene 5 (0:21-0:26): A phone and laptop side-by-side, both showing the same CV Central dashboard responsively — the phone screen shows a smooth install prompt sliding up from the bottom of the screen with an app icon. Text overlay: "Free to start. Works everywhere."

Scene 6 (0:26-0:30): Final logo reveal — the CV Central wordmark (white text, lime-green "Central") assembles on screen with a soft violet glow behind it, a lime-green underline sweeps in beneath the tagline. Text overlay: "CV Central — Build. Score. Land." Fade to black.

## Shorter version (for tools with a hard ~10-15s limit)

A 10-second vertical product demo video for "CV Central," an AI CV builder. Dark near-black background, violet (#5B2D8E) and lime-green (#AAFF00) accents only, clean modern sans-serif UI. Open on a laptop screen showing a CV score ring animating from 0 to 92 in a bold violet ring with a monospace number counter. Cut to AI-generated bullet text typing itself onto a CV in real time with a lime-green highlight sweep. End on the CV Central logo assembling with a soft glow and the tagline "Build. Score. Land." fading in beneath it. Upbeat minimal electronic music, brisk pacing, smooth camera push-ins, no shaky handheld motion.

---

## Notes for reuse

- Swap "78" / "92" for whatever score feels most persuasive — keep it in the 70s-90s range so it reads as achievable but impressive.
- If the tool struggles rendering actual legible UI text, describe it more abstractly (e.g., "a ring of light filling clockwise" instead of naming exact UI elements) — most text-to-video models fumble small on-screen text and numbers.
- The brand only uses two accent colors (violet + lime) against near-black or white — repeating that constraint in every scene description keeps the output visually consistent instead of drifting into generic stock-video colors.
