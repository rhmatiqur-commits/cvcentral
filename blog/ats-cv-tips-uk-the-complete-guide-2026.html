ATS CV Tips UK: The Complete Guide (2026)

CV Central 05 August 2026 7 min read

What Is ATS CV Tips UK?

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software applications that help employers sort and filter through job applications and resumes. In the UK, organisations of all sizes increasingly utilise ATS to streamline their hiring processes. ATS CV tips UK are essential guidelines designed to equip job seekers with the skills needed to craft a resume that is compatible with these systems.

An ATS scans resumes for specific keywords and information that match the employer's criteria. This means that even if you are the perfect candidate, your CV could be automatically rejected if it does not meet the system's requirements. Therefore, understanding and applying ATS CV tips UK can significantly impact your job search outcomes.

In this guide, we will explore best ATS CV tips UK, how to get started, and common mistakes to avoid. Whether you are a seasoned professional or a beginner, this comprehensive overview will provide you with the necessary tools to navigate the ATS successfully.

Why ATS CV Tips UK Matters

Understanding ATS CV tips UK is crucial for several reasons. First and foremost, statistics show that approximately 98% of Fortune 500 companies use ATS to manage their recruitment processes. This means that if you’re applying to well-established companies in the UK, your CV will likely be assessed by an ATS before it reaches the hiring manager.

Moreover, the competition for jobs today is tougher than ever. A well-structured CV that adheres to ATS requirements significantly enhances your chances of standing out in a saturated job market. An ATS-friendly CV not only ensures your application passes through the initial screening but also improves the likelihood of being reviewed by a recruiter or hiring manager, thereby increasing your chances of landing interviews.

Furthermore, many job roles in various sectors require specific skills and experiences. ATS helps companies ensure that applicants meet these criteria. Thus, using ATS CV tips UK will demonstrate to potential employers that you can follow instructions and take the application process seriously—qualities that are often sought after in candidates.

How to Get Started with ATS CV Tips UK

Starting your journey toward creating an ATS-friendly CV can be broken down into several actionable steps.

  1. Understand the Job Requirements: Before tailoring your CV, carefully read the job description. Identify keywords and phrases that describe the ideal candidate. Pay attention to both required skills and preferred qualifications listed in the job posting.
  1. Choose the Right Format: Use a clean, professional format. Common ATS-friendly formats are chronological and functional. Avoid complex layouts with tables, columns, or graphics as these can confuse ATS parsing.
  1. Incorporate Relevant Keywords: Use the keywords and phrases identified in step one throughout your CV. This includes your professional summary, experience, and skills sections. However, ensure the integration of these keywords feels natural and does not compromise the readability of your CV.
  1. Use Standard Job Titles and Descriptions: Avoid using unconventional job titles or descriptions that might not be recognised by the ATS. Stick to widely used terms that accurately reflect your experience.
  1. Format Your CV Appropriately: It's essential to save your CV in a preferred format such as .docx or .pdf, as specified by the employer, since some ATS can only parse certain formats correctly.
By following these initial steps, you'll be well on your way to creating a CV that both ATS and recruiters will appreciate.

Best Practices for ATS CV Tips UK

While creating an ATS-friendly CV, adhering to best practices can further enhance your chances. Here are key practices to ensure your CV captures the attention of both ATS and hiring managers:

  1. Craft a Targeted CV: Each job application should have a tailored CV. Modify your professional summary and experience sections according to the specific job description you're applying for. This targeted approach will minimise the risk of your CV being flagged as irrelevant.
  1. Prioritise Your Experience: List your work experience in reverse chronological order, focusing on relevant roles. Use bullet points for clarity and begin each point with strong action verbs (e.g., “Developed,” “Managed,” “Led”).
  1. Highlight Achievements: Instead of merely listing job responsibilities, emphasise accomplishments with quantifiable results where possible. For example, “Increased sales by 20% within one year” provides a clearer picture of your impact rather than a generic task description.
  1. Use Simple Fonts and Formatting: Stick to standard, easy-to-read fonts such as Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman in 10-12 points. Avoid excessive use of bold, italics, and different colours. This ensures that ATS and human reviewers can easily read your CV.
  1. Include Contact Information: Place your name, phone number, email address, and LinkedIn profile (if applicable) at the top of your CV. Ensure this information is up-to-date to facilitate direct communication.
  1. Proofread for Errors: Spelling and grammatical errors can lead to automatic disqualification by ATS. Use grammar checks and ask someone to review your CV for mistakes.
By incorporating these best ATS CV tips UK into your resume creation process, you can significantly increase your chances of success.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

When crafting your CV, certain common pitfalls can hinder your chances of passing the ATS screening process. Here are mistakes to avoid:

  1. Ignoring ATS Compatibility: Many job seekers are unaware of how ATS works. Submitting creatively formatted CVs filled with images, graphics, and intricate designs can lead to rejection. Always test for ATS compatibility.
  1. Keyword Stuffing: While incorporating keywords is vital, overstuffing them can lead to negative impressions. ATS systems are sophisticated enough to identify unnatural use of keywords, which can signal to employers that you may not be genuine.
  1. Omitting Core Skills: Tailor your skills section to match the qualifications and requirements stated in the job description. Failing to list critical skills relevant to the role can result in your CV being dismissed.
  1. Using Uncommon Job Titles: Maintaining standard terminology is crucial. Avoid using niche titles that may not be recognised by ATS. If you held a unique title, include the standard industry equivalent in brackets.
  1. Neglecting the Cover Letter: A well-crafted cover letter complements your CV. If the job description asks for one, ensure you provide a concise and tailored cover letter that outlines why you are a good fit for the position.
By avoiding these common mistakes and understanding best practices, you position yourself as a competitive candidate in the UK job market.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is an ATS, and how does it work in UK job applications?

An ATS, or Applicant Tracking System, is software that streamlines the recruitment process. It scans CVs to filter and rank applicants based on criteria established by the employer. In the UK, many organisations utilise ATS to manage their candidate pool, making it imperative for job seekers to optimise their CVs accordingly.

2. How can I ensure my CV is ATS-friendly?

To make your CV ATS-friendly, use standard fonts, avoid complex layouts, and tailor your content with keywords and phrases relevant to the job description. Stick to traditional formatting, include headings and bullet points, and save your document in an appropriate format (usually .docx or .pdf) as specified by the employer.

3. Are there specific keywords I should include in my CV?

Yes, specific keywords often come from the job description. Pay close attention to required skills, qualifications, and industry-specific terms. Integration of these keywords throughout your CV—particularly in your summary and experience sections—is crucial for ATS compliance.

4. Is it necessary to tailor my CV for every job application?

Yes, tailoring your CV for each job application significantly boosts your chances of success. Customising your summary and experience sections to align with the job description maximises compatibility with ATS and demonstrates your genuine interest in the role.

5. What is the best format for an ATS-friendly CV?

The best format for an ATS-friendly CV is a simple, clean layout, often in chronological or functional styles. Avoid headers and footers, use standard fonts, and ensure the document avoids complicated formatting to improve ATS parsing.

6. How can I check if my CV is ATS-friendly?

You can use online tools and software designed to evaluate CVs for ATS compatibility. Additionally, you can copy and paste your CV into a plain text document to see how it appears without formatting, which can give you insight into how an ATS may read it.

7. Can I use a resume template for my ATS CV?

Yes, using a resume template can help, but it’s essential to choose one that is ATS-friendly. Opt for templates that do not include excessive graphics, tables, or columns and that ensure a straightforward layout for better parsing by ATS.

In conclusion, utilising these ATS CV tips UK will better prepare you for today’s job market, enhancing your chances of making it through to interviews and ultimately securing the job you desire.
