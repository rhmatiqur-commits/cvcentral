01The problemBefore a human reads a word

Rejected before a human even sees your CV.

Recruitment is filtered by software before it is judged by people. Applicant tracking systems parse your CV first, recruiters give what survives a few seconds, and a generic application reads exactly like the two hundred sent before it.

In play Through Filtered out

Illustrative — the shape of a hiring funnel, not measured data.

  1. 01

    Applications sent

    You apply. So does everyone else. Volume is the entire problem — and it is not solved by applying harder.

  2. 02

    ATS filtering

    Software parses your file before a person does. Columns it can't read, headings it doesn't recognise, terminology that doesn't match the advert — the application ends here, silently.

  3. 03

    Recruiter screening

    What survives gets seconds of human attention. Responsibilities without outcomes, and outcomes without numbers, get skimmed straight past.

  4. 04

    Interview

    The few that read as an obvious match for this role — not any role — make it into the room.

The filter isn’t judging you. It’s judging your file.

06Interview preparationRehearse before it counts

The application doesn’t end with the invitation.

Getting the interview is the halfway point. CV Central generates the questions this role is likely to ask, helps you structure real answers from your own experience, and tells you where they’re thin.

Interviewer

“Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership.”

Your answer

“When our channel budget was cut mid-year, I took the team through a reallocation plan — reprioritised four markets, moved spend into organic, and kept everyone briefed weekly. We came in under budget and pipeline still grew.”

AI feedback

Situation & task are clear Strong
Action is specific and yours Strong
Result needs a number Fix this

“Under budget” and “still grew” are the weakest words in a strong answer. Say by how much.
